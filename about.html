Our History

COLEFIT was established in 2023 with the aim of creating a gym where people could improve their health, gain confidence, and support one another throughout their fitness journey.

The idea behind the gym was to create a friendly environment where people of all fitness levels could work towards their goals while motivating one another.

Inspired by the motto “Drop the Phones. Pick Up the Bells.”, COLEFIT encourages members to stay focused, live healthier lives, and grow stronger together as a community.

Our Mission

To help people of all fitness levels improve their health by providing a welcoming environment, quality equipment, and the support they need to achieve their fitness goals.

We believe that every small step leads to lasting results.

Our Vision

To become a trusted fitness community that inspires people to live healthier, more confident, and active lives.

Our goal is to make fitness a part of everyday life for everyone.

Our Values

  • Community
  • Health
  • Confidence
  • Motivation
  • Support
